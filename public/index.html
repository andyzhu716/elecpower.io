Isolated Phase Bus (IPB)

Metal-enclosed busbar with isolated phase conductors for high-current generator connections.

  • Current ratings up to 40,000A
  • Phase-separated enclosures for fault protection
  • Aluminum or copper conductors
Learn More

Non-Segregated Phase Bus (NSPB)

Cost-effective enclosed busbar for medium-voltage distribution in power plants and industrial facilities.

  • Aluminum alloy conductors
  • Compact switchgear integration
  • IEC 62271-215 compliant
Learn More

Cast Resin Busbar

Factory-sealed busbar systems for harsh environments — outdoor, humid, and chemically aggressive conditions.

  • Epoxy resin encapsulation, IP68 rated
  • Suitable for outdoor and marine environments
  • High chemical and moisture resistance

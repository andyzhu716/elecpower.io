## About This Site

ElecPower is an independent technical resource for busbar system selection and application. The goal is to provide clear, practical engineering reference material for professionals evaluating and specifying busbar systems.

**Not a manufacturer. Not a sales channel.** All information reflects general industry knowledge and publicly available standards.

## Technical Scope

The site covers the following busbar system types:

- **IPB (Isolated Phase Bus)** — High-current generator connections, typically 5,000A–40,000A
- **NSPB (Non-Segregated Phase Bus)** — Medium-voltage enclosed busbar, typically up to 10,000A  
- **Cast Resin Busbar** — Sealed busbar for harsh environments, IP68 rated
- **LV Busbar Trunking (LFM/LGB)** — Low-voltage distribution busbar per IEC 61439-6

## Content Approach

Technical articles are written from an engineering perspective — focusing on selection criteria, thermal design, short-circuit withstand, and application suitability. Where possible, references are made to IEC standards and industry practices.

Content is maintained independently without manufacturer sponsorship.

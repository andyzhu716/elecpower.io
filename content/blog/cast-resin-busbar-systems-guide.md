# Cast Resin Busbar Systems: Complete Technical Guide and Selection

Cast resin busbar — commonly referred to as **浇筑母线** in Chinese technical literature — is a fully encapsulated busbar system where each phase conductor is individually cast in epoxy resin, creating a monolithic insulated conductor that requires no additional external insulation.

Cast resin busbar is the premium choice for environments where moisture, corrosive chemicals, dust, or space constraints make conventional air-insulated or oil-filled busbar systems impractical. This article provides engineers with a complete technical reference covering principle, advantages, typical applications, specifications, and selection guidance.

---

## 1. What Is Cast Resin Busbar?

Cast resin busbar consists of copper or aluminium conductors individually encapsulated in thermosetting epoxy resin (typically F-type or B-type epoxy). The casting process is performed under vacuum to eliminate voids and gas pockets, achieving a void-free insulation bond between conductor and resin.

The resulting product is a compact, fully insulated rigid busbar with the following characteristics:

- **No additional insulation needed** — the epoxy IS the insulation
- **Phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth separation built into the casting**
- **IP68 rating achievable** — fully dust-tight and submersible
- **Corrosion-proof** — immune to salt spray, chemicals, humidity

### 1.1 Epoxy Resin Formulations

The two primary epoxy resin systems used in cast resin busbar:

| Resin Type | Properties | Typical Use |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| F-type epoxy (thermosetting) | Excellent electrical properties, fire retardant (IEC 60332), rated Class F (155°C) | Power plants, industrial |
| B-type epoxy (room temperature cure) | Lower cost, suitable for prefabricated sections | Commercial buildings, indoor |

### 1.2 Comparison with Alternative Busbar Types

For a comprehensive comparison across all busbar types, see our guide: [Where Are Busbar Systems Commonly Used?](/blog/busbar-system-applications/)

Key differentiators of cast resin busbar:

| Feature | Cast Resin | Air-insulated NSPB | IPB |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|-----|
| Insulation | Epoxy (solid) | Air | Air (in isolated enclosures) |
| IP rating | Up to IP68 | IP54–IP65 | IP65 |
| Phase clearance | Built into casting | Requires spacing | Individual enclosures |
| Corrosion resistance | Excellent | Moderate | Good |
| Installation space | Compact (60–70% of air-insulated) | Large | Very large |
| Cost | High | Medium | Highest |
| Maintenance | Very low | Moderate | Moderate |

## 2. Key Advantages of Cast Resin Busbar

### 2.1 Space Saving

Because phase conductors are fully encapsulated with solid insulation, cast resin busbar requires approximately **40–60% less installation space** compared to equivalent air-insulated busbar systems. This is critical in:

- Urban substations with limited footprint
- Offshore platforms
- Ships and vessels
- Retrofitting existing switchgear rooms

### 2.2 Superior Environmental Protection

Cast resin busbar achieves IP68 — the highest protection level in the busbar family. It is specifically designed for:

- **Salt spray environments** — coastal substations, offshore platforms
- **Chemical plants** — sulfuric acid, chlorine, ammonia atmospheres
- **Dusty environments** — cement plants, flour mills
- **Humid/tropical climates** — no condensation risk inside the casting

For condensation prevention in less-sealed alternatives, see: [How to Prevent Condensation in Enclosed Busbar Systems](/blog/how-to-prevent-condensation-in-enclosed-busbar-systems/)

### 2.3 Fire Safety

Modern cast resin busbar uses fire-retardant epoxy resin rated to **IEC 60332** and classified as **Class F insulation (155°C thermal rating)**. Some products also comply with **IEC 60694** for fire-withstand testing.

### 2.4 Low Maintenance

Once installed, cast resin busbar requires minimal maintenance:

- No periodic cleaning of insulator surfaces
- No concern for animal/insect intrusion
- No UV degradation (fully enclosed)
- Service life typically exceeds 30 years

## 3. Technical Specifications

### 3.1 Electrical Specifications

| Parameter | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Rated voltage | 0.66 kV – 35 kV |
| Rated current | 400A – 6,300A |
| Dielectric strength | ≥ 20 kV/mm (epoxy) |
| Partial discharge | ≤ 10 pC at rated voltage |
| Tan δ (dissipation factor) | ≤ 0.008 at 20°C |
| Short-circuit withstand (1s) | 25 kA – 50 kA |
| Peak withstand | 63 kA – 125 kA |

### 3.2 Mechanical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Conductor material | Copper (99.9%) or aluminium |
| Resin material | Epoxy resin, F-class |
| Thermal expansion coefficient | ~12×10⁻⁶/°C (similar to copper) — minimises delamination |
| Adherence (conductor–resin) | ≥ 10 N/mm² (shear bond) |
| Flame propagation | IEC 60332-3 (Category A) |
| Oxygen index | ≥ 30% |

### 3.3 Dimensional Reference

A typical 10 kV, 2,000A cast resin busbar section:

| Dimension | Typical Value |
|-----------|--------------|
| Phase width | 80–100 mm |
| Phase height | 60–80 mm |
| Phase spacing | 30–50 mm |
| Wall thickness (resin) | 6–10 mm |
| Weight (copper) | ~15–20 kg/m |

## 4. Applications

### 4.1 Coastal and Offshore Substations

Cast resin busbar is the preferred choice in coastal substations where salt spray would rapidly degrade conventional air-insulated busbar. Typical installations include:

- 110 kV/35 kV coastal transformer substations
- Offshore oil platform electrical rooms
- Port cargo handling facilities

### 4.2 Chemical and Petrochemical Plants

In chemical plants where acidic or alkaline vapours are present, cast resin busbar provides reliable long-term performance without the maintenance burden of sealed air-insulated systems.

### 4.3 Power Plants and Switchgear Rooms

Cast resin busbar is used within switchgear lineup connections and for transformer-to-switchgear busbars in areas with high humidity or dust.

For power plant busbar selection more broadly: [How to Choose the Right Busbar System for a Power Plant](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-busbar-system-for-a-power-plant/)

### 4.4 Ships and Marine Vessels

The vibration resistance, space efficiency, and corrosion-proof characteristics of cast resin busbar make it the standard choice for shipboard power distribution systems.

## 5. Installation Requirements

### 5.1 Support and Securing

Cast resin busbar requires careful support design:

- **Support spacing**: Typically 1,000–1,500 mm (shorter than NSPB due to rigidity)
- **Support clamps**: Non-magnetic stainless steel (to avoid eddy current heating)
- **Alignment**: Expansion allowance must be provided at one end (typically 3–5 mm per metre of run)
- **Penetration sealing**: Fire-rated sealant required at wall/floor penetrations

### 5.2 Termination and Jointing

Cast resin busbar sections are factory-fabricated to length. Jointing in the field uses:

- **Bolted flat-plate joints** with silver-plated contact surfaces
- **Epoxy pouring kits** for field-made terminations
- **Heat-shrink terminations** for cable transitions

### 5.3 Testing Before Energisation

Mandatory pre-commissioning tests for cast resin busbar:

1. **Visual inspection** — check for resin chips, cracks, delamination at joints
2. **Resistance measurement** — all joints and terminations (micro-ohm level)
3. **Insulation resistance test** — ≥ 100 MΩ at 1 kV DC
4. **Voltage withstand** — 2.5× rated voltage AC for 1 minute
5. **Partial discharge test** — ≤ 10 pC at rated voltage

## 6. Selection Guide

Use this checklist when specifying cast resin busbar:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Voltage class | Match system voltage (0.66/6/10/24/35 kV) |
| Current rating | Design current × 1.15 minimum |
| Short-circuit rating | ≥ system fault level (typically 31.5 kA or 40 kA for 10 kV) |
| IP rating | IP68 for outdoor/harsh; IP54 for indoor |
| Resin class | F-class (155°C) minimum for power plant |
| Fire rating | IEC 60332 compliant for public buildings |
| Standards | IEC 62271-200, GB/T 12777, or equivalent |
| Manufacturer type tests | Required from accredited laboratory |
| Thermal expansion compatibility | Verify with adjacent switchgear/transformer |
